# Proposed sprint plan — South America Carnival

**Status:** Retrospective Scrum-inspired case study; no historical formal Scrum ceremonies or staffed team claimed.

| Increment | Sprint goal | Backlog focus | Inspectable outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 — Discover | Define the product goal | PB-01, PB-02 | Vision, constraints, initial priorities |
| 1 — Design | Build a workable route | PB-02, PB-05, PB-06, PB-08 | Sequence, overnight bases, day trips, critical dependencies |
| 2 — Prepare | Make the travel baseline actionable | PB-03, PB-09 | Readiness checklist, essential booking plan, contingencies |
| 3 — Carnival | Deliver the primary travel value | PB-01, PB-04 | São Paulo opening and twelve-day Rio experience |
| 4 — Regional contrast | Cover Uruguay and initial Buenos Aires | PB-05, PB-06 | Montevideo base, Punta day trip, bus/ferry connection |
| 5 — Adventure and return | Complete Patagonia, Iguazú, Paraguay, São Paulo | PB-06, PB-07, PB-08, PB-09 | Southern and northern experiences, viable homebound route |
| 6 — Review | Publish auditable case-study evidence | PB-10 | Case narrative, risk table, backlog, links to visual portfolio |

## Definition of Ready
Outcome and value are clear; destination or dependency known; critical date risk understood; evidence or acceptance approach stated.

## Definition of Done
Experience documented as delivered, consciously adapted or outside scope. No claim of historical task completion or metrics without corresponding evidence.

## Inspection and adaptation
At each meaningful stage, revisit remaining time, next departure, weather-dependent activities and remaining highest-value experiences. This is a suggested lightweight travel practice, not a record of formal Daily Scrums.

## Retrospective
Keep one strong product goal and efficient day-trip design. Improve documented cost tracking and decisions. Experiment with rolling 48-hour planning on future journeys.
